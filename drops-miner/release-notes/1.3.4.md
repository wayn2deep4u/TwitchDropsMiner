## Bonkr Twitch Miner 1.3.4

**Fix: the app froze when signing in to Kick.** On Windows, opening the Kick sign-in window could lock up the whole app (white Kick window, main window stuck at "Updating"). The sign-in window was being built in the middle of the button click, which Windows can't handle; it's now built just after. Only one Kick page runs at a time, too: while you sign in, the miner's hidden Kick page is closed, and it comes back once you close the sign-in window. The Twitch sign-in window had the same flaw and is fixed the same way.

- Signing in to Kick: log in on the Kick window, then close it. Kick mining starts a few seconds later.
- If something on the Kick side stalls, the app writes what it was doing to `kick-debug.log` in its data folder (Settings › Open data folder).

Bonkr Twitch Miner is a Rust/Tauri port of [DevilXD's Twitch Drops Miner](https://github.com/DevilXD/TwitchDropsMiner). All credit for the original goes to DevilXD.
