**Drops Miner** mines Twitch drops in the background on Windows. It's a Rust and Tauri rebuild of [TwitchDropsMiner by DevilXD](https://github.com/DevilXD/TwitchDropsMiner) with a new window: Overview, Channels, Inventory, Activity, Settings and Account.

## Download

Download **Drops.Miner_1.1.1_x64-setup.exe** under **Assets** below (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit, about 3 MB).

1. Run the installer. Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"** because the app isn't code-signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
2. Open Drops Miner and sign in to Twitch in the window that opens. Only the sign-in token is kept, on your PC.
3. Go to **Settings** and add the games you want drops for, most wanted first. Then click **Save and reload**.

It keeps mining in the tray when you close the window. Quit from the tray icon.

## What it does

- Picks the best live channel for your games and switches when a better one goes live
- Follows drop progress live and claims every drop as soon as it's ready, with a Windows notification
- **Featured for you**: the next rewards for your games, right on the Overview page
- Pin a channel with **Watch**, or let the miner choose
- Your game lists are also backed up to `Documents\Drops Miner`, so they survive reinstalls

Like the original, it never downloads video. It only asks Twitch for each stream's playlist, so it uses almost no bandwidth.

## Good to know

- Twitch only counts one channel at a time per account, so the miner watches one channel.
- Campaigns that need a linked game account show **Link account** until you link it.
- Twitch changes its private API every so often. When the original miner gets a fix, this one gets an update.
