## Bonkr Twitch Miner 1.3.2

**Fix: the app froze when signing in to Kick.** On Windows, opening the Kick sign-in window could lock up the whole app (white Kick window, main window stuck at "Updating"). The app now reads your Kick sign-in without the call that caused the lock-up.

Bonkr Twitch Miner is a Rust/Tauri port of [DevilXD's Twitch Drops Miner](https://github.com/DevilXD/TwitchDropsMiner). All credit for the original goes to DevilXD.
